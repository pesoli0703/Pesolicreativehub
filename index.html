Frequently Asked Questions

Can blurry photos be restored? +

Yes! Our advanced AI technology and retouching expertise can significantly improve blur. While we can't make a severely blurry photo crystal clear, we can enhance clarity, reduce noise, and make it sharp enough for beautiful prints.

Can damaged photos be repaired? +

Absolutely! We specialize in restoring torn, scratched, water-damaged, and faded photographs. Our team uses professional retouching techniques to repair damage while maintaining the original character of your photo.

Can phone pictures look like studio portraits? +

Definitely! Our Studio Portrait Transformation service uses professional lighting adjustments, background enhancement, and premium retouching to make casual phone photos look like they were taken in a professional studio.

How long does editing take? +

Most projects are completed within 24–48 hours. Complex restorations may take longer depending on the damage level. We'll give you an exact timeline when you send your photo.

How do I place an order? +

Simply send your photo on WhatsApp to 08109615982. Describe what you want, and we'll respond with a personalized assessment and quote. Once you approve, we start working on your masterpiece!

How do I receive my final design? +

Your finished files will be sent via WhatsApp in the highest resolution. You can download them immediately and use them for printing, framing, social media, or however you like!
